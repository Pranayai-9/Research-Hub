## 2. Research Overview

### Problem Statement
The recurring problem researchers tend to shed light and work on was lack of a proper structred framework and organized approach to read a research paper, causing frustration, inability to extract data insights and gradual inaccess to papers over ignorance by students and other novices.

### Research Motivation
Research is very important domain of data analysis, extraction and understanding. Research papers serve as a standard, well-documented emperical evidence based insights, reviews, studies over various domains, themes, systems and other complex theoretical and practical knowledge. Inability to properly infer from such quality resources due to lack of a structured framework hinder the scope of acedmic excellence and enthusiasm. Many problem statements need clear and well-documented literature survey to understand and investigate the exsisting systems and methodolgies established.The aim to provide a framework solving this comprhension bottleneck is valuable and ambitious.

### Research Objectives
The researchers are trying to achieve a structured framework, organized approach and a method that can be followed to understand, diagnoze and reproduce a research paper productively.

## 3. Research Methodology

### Proposed Approach
The researcher proposed a "Three-pass" structure where the process of reading a research paper is divided into 3 structural phases.
Phase 1: 
This phase is served as the approach based on the bird-eye-view approach where the aim is to find out the general idea of the paper, understand the scope and outline developed. It serves as phase where reader tends to get a brief idea on what type of content he is gonna read further with some important fundamental framework details.
This phase involves answering to questions like?
1.What is the problem statement?
2.Why is this problem neccessary?
3.What is the researcher's approach used?
4.What is the main contribution observed?
Completing this phase gives a clear idea of what is the core thing discussed and analysed by the researcher in the paper to the reader.If reader feels a mounting pressure of technical jargoans causing unfamiliar concepts at front, he may skip reading it further.

Phase 2:
This is the body of the 3-phase structure where the reader gets to learn,read and understand the entire contents of the paper.Reader tend to understand the methodologies, frameworks, approaches, evidence-based insights, cause-and-effects discussed mainly focusing on the root-diagnosis of problem statement and solution hypothesis proposed rather than the technical evidence and information.This phase gives idea about the following themes:
1.Category- type of research
2.Context - The background information relating to the theme discussed
3.Correctness - validating the accuracy using statistical evidence
4.Contribution - What is the major proposed value creation through the paper?
5.Clarity - How clear is the assumption and proposed verified solutions based on insights.

Phase 3:
The important phase requiring higher attention-in-detail, involving recreation of the paper contents, adding own insights, finding gaps, limitations, verifying assumptions, analysing hypothesis, criticising unclear assumptions, and retrospecting own understanding of the problem statement and solution discussed.
